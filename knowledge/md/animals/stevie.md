# Stevie

- **IRI:** `https://example.org/ontology/pets#Stevie`
- **Types:** [Cat](../vocab/species-and-breeds.md#cat)

Five-year-old female cow-pattern (black-and-white bicolor) cat. Extremely shy with strangers. Energetic and playful. She loves her feather wand and drags it to a person when she wants to play. She loves Temptations treats and Aussie wet food. Fleece is her favorite fabric; she is more inclined to be affectionate if a fleece blanket or robe is handy.

## Anti-bids

**Forced approach while shy** (`pets:ForcedApproachWhileShy`). Extremely shy with strangers — do not rush or force contact. Quiet approach; fleece blanket or robe handy.

**No-go body regions:** unknown — do not invent them. After a protest: give space, offer a treat, or sit quietly without reaching. Household warning signals apply.

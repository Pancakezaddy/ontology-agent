# Salem

- **IRI:** `https://example.org/ontology/pets#Salem`
- **Types:** [Cat](../vocab/species-and-breeds.md#cat)

Five-year-old female black cat. She readily presents her belly. She loves vigorous belly rubs and light chin and cheek scratches. She is warm and affectionate when she comes out of her bat cave. Biggest motivations are the feather wand toy and Temptations treats. She can be found scaling Christmas trees and loves swinging around the cat-tree pole. She typically needs at least six hours to warm up to people unless a tub of Temptations is shaken. Bids include sprawling on a person's lap.

## Anti-bids

**Approaching during warm-up** (`pets:ApproachingDuringWarmUp`). Do not push contact in the first ~6 hours with a new person unless you shake a tub of Temptations.

No no-go body regions asserted. After a protest: give space, offer a treat, or sit quietly without reaching. Household warning signals apply.

# Q

- **IRI:** `https://example.org/ontology/pets#Q`
- **Types:** [Rabbit](../vocab/species-and-breeds.md#rabbit), [Dutch rabbit](../vocab/species-and-breeds.md#dutch-rabbit)

Three-year-old male Dutch rabbit. Confident but nervous and shy at first. Does not love being touched unless he invited it. When he wants pets he flops nearby or loafs, makes eye contact, and chitters his teeth. Loves treats and dry food. Loves a sunny windowsill. Not a big fan of stairs. Sometimes upset when Jules blocks him from eating or steals his treats.

Shares a room with [Jules](jules.md) (housing footnote).

## Anti-bids

**Uninvited touch** (`pets:UninvitedTouch`). Do not touch him unless he consented (flop nearby, or loaf + eye contact + teeth chitter). **All touch is off-limits until he invites it** — no named no-go region beyond that.

**Being picked up** / **being scruffed** (`pets:BeingPickedUp`, `pets:BeingScruffed`). Do not, except a **vet emergency**.

He is sometimes upset when Jules blocks food or steals treats. After a protest: give space, offer a treat, or sit quietly without reaching. Household warning signals apply.

# Lilo

- **IRI:** `https://example.org/ontology/pets#Lilo`
- **Types:** [Cat](../vocab/species-and-breeds.md#cat)

Female calico cat, around ten years old. Strongly motivated by chicken and Aussie wet food; wet food is her primary affection motivation. Not strongly motivated by Temptations but will accept them. Prefers to sit awkwardly at a distance and head-bonks when she wants pets. Warm-up to strangers is under an hour.

## Anti-bids

**Crowding while distant** (`pets:CrowdingWhileDistant`). Awkward sit-at-distance is her posture, not an invitation to close in. Wait for a **head bonk** before pets.

**No-go body regions:** unknown — do not invent them. After a protest: give space, offer a treat, or sit quietly without reaching. Household warning signals apply.

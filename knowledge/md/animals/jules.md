# Jules

- **IRI:** `https://example.org/ontology/pets#Jules`
- **Types:** [Rabbit](../vocab/species-and-breeds.md#rabbit), [Rex rabbit](../vocab/species-and-breeds.md#rex-rabbit)

Two-year-old female Rex rabbit. Affectionate, warm, and hungry. Primarily food motivated; loves to eat. Will try to block Q from eating and steal his treats; Q is sometimes upset by this. Most content with her feet sticking out. Loves rubs on her head, neck, and shoulder, and gentle kneading on her back and side belly.

Shares a room with [Q](q.md) (housing footnote).

## Anti-bids

**Food blocking of cagemate** (`pets:FoodBlockingOfCagemate`) toward [Q](q.md). She may block him from eating and steal treats; he is sometimes upset. This is not a human affection bid.

**Uninvited touch** (`pets:UninvitedTouch`). Wait for a bid.

**Ventral belly touch** (`pets:VentralBellyTouch`). Belly is a no-go unless she has invited **gentle side-belly kneading** (not full ventral handling).

**Feet tucked means stop** (`pets:FeetTuckedMeansStop`). Feet sticking out: continue. Feet tucked in: stop pets.

**Being picked up** / **being scruffed**. Do not pick up or scruff except a **vet emergency**. Extra pickup exception: a handler who is confident and can keep her **fully supported**.

After a protest: give space, offer a treat, or sit quietly without reaching. Household warning signals apply.

# Food and treats

- **Food** — meal food, distinct from a treat
- **Treat** — reward / attention food
- **hasFavoredFood**, **hasFavoredTreat**
- **hasPrimaryMotivation** — strongest day-to-day motivation (toy or food)
- **hasLowMotivationFor** / **willAccept** — e.g. Lilo and Temptations
